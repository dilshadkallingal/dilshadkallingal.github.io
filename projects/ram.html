Ram Clinics

Appointment booking app for a healthcare provider in Saudi Arabia.

Period
May 2023 – Nov 2023
Company
Zensign LLP
Team
3 developers
Stack
Flutter, Dart, Firebase, Provider, REST APIs

The app turned the clinic group's website into a mobile experience. Patients can book services across multiple branches, pick a doctor and time slot, manage their bookings and pay in instalments.

Features

  • Booking across multiple clinic locations
  • Branch, doctor and time slot selection
  • Instalment payments through integrated providers
  • Real-time booking status and appointment details
  • One codebase for iOS and Android

My role

  • Led the technical implementation and architecture.
  • Built the booking, calendar and payment modules.
  • Integrated Firebase Authentication and Firestore for real-time appointment data.
  • Used Provider for state management and tuned performance.
  • Handled App Store and Google Play releases.

What I learned

Designing a complex booking UI in Flutter, integrating instalment payment providers, and delivering a secure healthcare app with a small team.
