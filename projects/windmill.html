Windmill Cellar

Product discovery and pickup ordering app for a UAE retailer.

Period
Oct 2022 – Apr 2023
Company
Devmonkey Inc
Team
2 developers
Stack
Flutter, Dart, Firebase, Riverpod, REST APIs, Bitrise

Windmill Cellar lets customers browse the store's catalogue, check stock, build a cart and find a nearby store for pickup. The app replaced a slower website experience and increased engagement.

Features

  • Search and filters for product discovery
  • Real-time inventory to avoid out-of-stock orders
  • Cart and product details before visiting the store
  • Store locator for pickup
  • Fast load times and smooth navigation

My role

  • Led the technical implementation and architecture.
  • Built the product catalogue and order tracking with Riverpod.
  • Integrated Firebase for real-time data.
  • Profiled and reduced rendering time, memory use and network calls.
  • Set up CI with Bitrise for automated testing and deployment.

What I learned

Advanced state management with Riverpod, performance profiling in Flutter, and how much responsiveness matters to users of a shopping app.
